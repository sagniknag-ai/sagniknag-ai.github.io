A little about me

Research with purpose.

Exploring drug delivery approaches and therapeutic candidates through pharmaceutical science and cancer research.

Sagnik Nag is a PhD researcher at Monash University Malaysia with research interests spanning nanomedicine, pharmaceutical sciences and molecular oncology.

His academic interests include the development and evaluation of drug delivery systems and the investigation of therapeutic candidates for cancer research.
